Standards & permanence

Stable routing + standardized astronomical fields.
  • QR codes resolve via /r/<ID> to the canonical record at /registry/<ID>.
  • Records present coordinates as RA (h m s) and Dec (° ′ ″), with epoch stated (e.g., J2000).
  • Catalog references (e.g., SAO) are stored as structured fields for future interoperability.

This is a private commemorative registry and is not affiliated with the International Astronomical Union (IAU).
